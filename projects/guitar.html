Electric Guitar

January 2026-May 2026

In high school I spent many nights learning and playing the guitar, but I wasn't able to bring my guitar to college. So, I decided to build my own!

I made the body of the guitar, and purchased the guitar neck and pickups. I chose to use two humbucker pickups for a warmer tone. The total project, including the electronics and a guitar amp, was $315; if you're curious, here's my BOM.

This project had a few main phases: ideation and design, woodworking, and electronics and debugging. Through this project I learned how much I love woodworking - there is something immensely satisfying and beautiful in carving out and creating from a block of wood! And guitar is even more enjoyable now that I can play my own guitar :)
