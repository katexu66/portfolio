MIT Motorsports: Driverless Mechanisms

June 2026 - Ongoing

This is my second year on MIT Motorsports. Last year I worked on power steering, and this year I'm Suspension Lead! As Suspension Lead, I'm leading a team of 11 engineers to design and fabricate the rockers, suspension links, and steering systems on the car. I own the technical milestones, timelines, and budgets across the subsystems, make go/no-go decisions (including, as of last week, a go on our first set of carbon fiber suspension links!), and provide technical guidance through all elements of the design cycle, including functional requirements, architecture selection, design, and manufacturing.

As Suspension Lead, I also worked with the rockers REs and VD lead to select and justify the development of a new suspension architecture this year—four-corner pushrod suspension with a rear anti-roll bar and third-element heave control—based on vehicle dynamics, packaging, mass, budget, and adjustability requirements.

So far we've finished setting the functional requirements for all of the suspension subsystems, decided on architectures, and are now beginning design. I can't wait for this season, and for the development of an amazing racecar suspension.
